SMS Communications

By submitting our contact form, you consent to receive SMS text messages from Alex D Plumbing at the phone number provided. Message frequency varies based on your service request — typically 2–4 messages per service interaction.

Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.

No Sharing of Mobile Numbers

TyAgent does not sell, rent, share, or disclose your mobile phone number to any third party for marketing or promotional purposes. Your phone number is used exclusively for service-related communications on behalf of the business whose form you submitted.

Data Security

TyAgent takes reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Your data is stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel for the purposes described above.
